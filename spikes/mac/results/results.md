## Recommended local defaults

Recommended local defaults on an M4 Pro 48 GB (measured 2026-11-12 to 2026-11-14): video LTX 2.3 distilled int4 through ltx-2-mlx, image FLUX.2 klein 4B through mflux, speech Kokoro 82M through mlx-audio, music MusicGen small (non-commercial weights; ACE-Step not tried yet). LTX is the only video model fast enough for a queue: about 2.8 min per 4 s clip at 832x448 with in-sync audio, against about 21 min for Wan 2.2 5B (no audio, ignores camera directions) and about an hour for Wan 2.1 1.3B (worst pictures). No local video model keeps a character's identity from text alone, so recurring characters need reference conditioning (plan 10b). Voice cloning works locally with F5-TTS but its weights are non-commercial.
